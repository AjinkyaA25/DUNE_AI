This document is unofficial and not created by Direwolf. However, it compiles official rulings from various sources in one easy to reference file. It contains every FAQ/Clarification from the official Dune FAQ and every rulebook, plus several additional questions and edge cases asked in the forums or Discord community.


Unless labeled otherwise, each entry has been verified by an official source or ruling from the designers. For any questions, comments, or additions, please message elessar on the TTS Club discord or on BGG.

GUIDING PRINCIPLES

Order of Operations
* On an Agent Turn, you:
1. Play a card from your hand to send the agent
2. Then place your agent on an eligible board location and pay the location cost (if any)
3. Pull a spy to Gather Intelligence if desired 
4. Then activate the effects of the board space and the agent box in any order (see below for details)
* Once you declare a Reveal Turn, you must immediately reveal your hand, and then can activate other effects in any order.
Source: Dune Imperium Rulebook 
Source: BGG forum
Source: Message from designer
   * Exception: See Guild Spy
        


Timing of Effects
   * As you take your turn, you may gain a variety of effects from board locations (including faction bumps), agent boxes, unload abilities, leader abilities, intrigue cards, tech tiles, contracts, and the research track. You may normally execute these effects in any order you choose, keeping in mind the restrictions below:
Source: Dune Imperium Rulebook 
      * You may not interrupt an “atomic effect” with another effect (see below)
      * If there is a [Cost → Reward] effect indicated by an arrow, you must perform the cost on the left before you perform the reward on the right.
Source: BGG Forum
      * You cannot activate an effect from a card that is already trashed
         * However, persuasion and swords go into a pool and don't go away even if the card that supplied them is trashed. This is true for your swords even if you started with no units in the combat.
See Duncan Loyal Blade
         * Unload effects are of course still activated as they are being trashed
         * Some effects are considered “already installed effects” and their effect can persist even after they are trashed. Examples include Weirding Way, Chairdog, and Usurp. Essentially the effect was already triggered by the card, before the card was destroyed.
Source: BGG Forum (Note: this ruling reverses a previous ruling) 
            * If you choose to recall a Spy to Gather Intelligence (draw a card), you must choose whether to do this immediately after placing your Agent, before receiving any effects of the board space or card you played.
Source: Uprising Rulebook 
               * Plot Intrigue Cards and relevant Tech Tiles may be played at any time on your Agent or Reveal turn, provided they abide by the restrictions above.
Source: Dune Imperium Rulebook 
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ
        
Atomic Effects
                  * An atomic effect is a grouping of effects considered to be one effect that cannot be interrupted. The following are atomic effects:
                  * Any double faction influence bump (e.g. Power Play, Shifting Allegiances) may not be interrupted.
Source: BGG Forum
                  * A [Cost → Reward] effect may not be interrupted. You must finish executing the full Cost and Reward before moving on to other effects.
                  * Exception: If the left side triggers any Unload effects, you MUST fully execute the Unload effects before moving on to the right side.
This is Paul’s most recent ruling - the question was previously answered differently:
Source: BGG Forum (2023) - You can’t interrupt it + exception with Unload
Source: BGG Forum (2022) - You can interrupt it
Source: BGG Forum (2021) - You can’t interrupt it
                  * Acquire Effects must be taken immediately upon acquiring the Card or Tech.
Source: Direwold Discord Post
                  * The effect “Choose Two:” (e.g on Long Reach, Machinations, Propaganda, Stitched Horror) must be chosen together and fully resolved before moving on to other effects. For example with Propaganda, you should announce which two factions you are moving up on before taking the bumps (if you reach BG Level 4 Influence, you don’t get to see the Intrigue before deciding your other bump).
Source: Message from designer
                     * If an Intrigue card has two effects (e.g. Depart for Arrakis), you may not normally interrupt the two effects with other player-initiated actions. Resolve the Intrigue card “in one piece” before doing other actions.
                     * Note that faction bonuses (such as drawing an Intrigue on the BG track) may interrupt this flow, since they are not considered player-initiated. See Changing Allegiances.
Source: Message from designer.
                     * In most cases, each sentence is considered a separate effect. 
                     * For example, Power Play’s two sentences are separate effects:
                     * “Trash this card.”
                     * “Gain two influence instead of one.”
Source: BGG Forum
                     * Exceptions:
                     * Imperium Ceremony’s two sentences are one effect.
Source: BGG forum
                     * Long Live the Fighter’s two sentences are one effect. 
Source: Message from designer


Mandatory vs. Optional Effects
                        * Effects in Dune Imperium (from board locations, Agent boxes, Reveal effects, Techs) are generally mandatory, with the following exceptions:
                        * When the word “may” is used. This includes:
                        * Tech Negotiation
                        * Acquire Tech
                        * The Black X trash icon
                        * Removing the Shield Wall token
                        * When “may” is printed on the card
                        * (Note that Fremen Bond and Command are mandatory, despite the word “may” mistakenly being used in the rulebook)
                        * Any time the effect is a cost for another reward (shown with an arrow)
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ
Source: Rise of Ix Rulebook 
Uprising Rulebook 
                        * You cannot execute & fizzle a conditional effect while the precondition is false. In other words, you can’t announce that you’re using the ability for no effect while the condition is false, to avoid using the effect when the condition becomes true.
                        * Example: You will be forced to draw a card from Prepare the Way if you reach (and maintain) 2 influence with the Bene Gesserit by the end of your turn. E.g. if you send Prepare the Way to High Council and fulfill the contract that gives you a Bene Gesserit influence. Unless you somehow drop back down below 2 influence by the end of your turn, you will be forced to activate the card draw from Prepare the Way.
                        * Example: Muad'Dib will be forced to draw an intrigue card from his passive ability if he has worms in the combat by the end of his Reveal turn. He can’t say he activated his passive before he plays Unexpected Allies to put in a worm.
Source: Hidden Assets Discord




Acquire Effects vs. Row Replacement
                        * Which happens “more immediately”: the row getting replaced with a new card/tech, or the Acquire effects from purchasing a card/tech?
The row getting replaced takes precedence, and should happen before you resolve the Acquire effect of your card or tech.
Source: Message from designer

Combat Phase Timing
                        * Is there a set order for taking combat rewards? The rules do not indicate an order for players to take their rewards, and in most cases it does not matter. If it does matter (e.g. someone has knowledge of the top Intrigue card), the designer recommends resolving in combat placement order (1st to 3rd), with the current round’s player turn order breaking ties if needed.
Source: Message from designer
                        * See this Quick Reference guide for a reminder on the timing within the Combat Phase, as well as tie scenarios.

  


FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS / CLARIFICATIONS


Alliance
                           * If you have an Alliance token and lose Influence with that Faction, you also lose the token in either of these cases:
                           * You were previously tied for Influence with another player; the player you were tied with now takes the Alliance token. (If you were tied with more than one other player, choose one to take it.)
                           * If you have fallen to 3 Influence or lower. (If no player has 4 Influence or higher to take the Alliance token, return it to the game board.)
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ


Appropriate
                           * You can’t mix Solari and spice to pay the cost of a Tech tile.
Source: Rise of Ix Rulebook  
                           * You can return Negotiators from Ix to reduce the cost by 1 Solari each.
Source: Rise of Ix Rulebook 
                           * If you send an activated Appropriate to shipping and drop from the top tier, you receive two distinct Acquire Tech effects:
                              * You may buy a tech with Spice, at a discount of 2 Spice. AND
                              * You may buy another tech using either Solari or Spice
You may spend Tech Negotiator cubes toward either transaction. You may NOT confuse the two transactions and receive a -2 discount on the tech you get from Appropriate.
Source: Direwolf Discord Post
Source: Rise of Ix Rulebook 


Archduke Armand Ecaz
                              * To use his “Coordination” ability, you must have at least two Agents on spaces with any of the three listed Agent icons. The Agents don’t need to be on spaces with the same icon.
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ
                              * If he controls the Mentat, it counts as one of his Agents and can help trigger the Coordination Ability.
Source: BGG Forum


Arrakis Revolt
                                 * If you visit a non-combat space with Arrakis Revolt (via grafting, e.g.), you may still deploy a sandworm to the combat.
Source: Message from designer


Assassination Mission
                                    * You must use some other means to trash this card for its 4 Solari; the card provides no way to trash itself.
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ


Baron Vladimir Harkonnen, Masterstroke
                                       * To use the Masterstroke ability, choose two of the four Baron Harkonnen tokens and place them on your Leader with the Faction side face down. (Return the other two to the box without letting any opponents see them.) Later, reveal your choices as described to gain your reward. You choose the factions for the “Masterstroke” ability after completing all setup, before the first round begins. You may only reveal your choices and gain Influence one time during a game.
Source: Dune Imperium Rulebook 
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ


Beguiling Pheromones
                                          * When you use this to trash the other grafted card, you can’t also get an effect on that card that requires trashing it as a cost. For example: when you use Beguiling Pheromones to trash a grafted Imperial Spy, you cannot also draw an Intrigue card using Imperial Spy (as trashing the card is a required cost for that effect).
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ
Source: BGG forum
                                          * You can use Beguiling Pheromones to trash a card that would otherwise be trashed anyway (for example: Foldspace, Seek Allies).
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ


Bindu Suspension
                                             * You must play this Intrigue card at the start of your Agent turn, before doing anything else. When you do, draw a card, then you may choose to pass your turn or play your turn normally.
Source: Dune Imperium Rulebook 
Source: BGG Forum (Note Paul’s correction on the language of the link above)

Bombast
                                                * If you trash Bombast with Calculus of Power, can you still get 3 solari from Bombast’s reveal effect? No. You cannot activate effects from a card that is already trashed. Either Calculus trashes Bombast, or Bombast trashes itself. You would need to choose whether you want 3 swords or (assuming you qualify for the Command effect) 3 solari.
See “Timing of Effects”
Source: BGG Forum 


Call to Arms
                                                   * “During your Reveal turn this round, whenever you acquire a card:” - this does not include Intrigue cards.
Source: Hidden Assets Discord post
                                                   * This Intrigue card does not work retroactively. To get any troops from it, you must play it before you acquire card/s.
Source: Hidden Assets Discord post
                                                      * You may play this card during an Agent Turn. If so, simply set it aside until your Reveal Turn.
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ (see Charisma)


Calculated Hire
                                                         * This Intrigue card can only take the Mentat from its starting position in the Landsraad.
Source: Dune Imperium Rulebook 


Carryall
                                                            * The base spice you harvest is 1 from Imperial Basin, 2 from Hagga Basin, and 3 from The Great Flat. (When playing Uprising, it is 2 from Habbanya Erg and 4 from Deep Desert.)
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ


Chairdog
                                                               * Once you return the other grafted card to your hand, you must complete your Reveal turn. You cannot change to an Agent turn and play the card you returned.
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ
                                                               * You can’t use Chairdog with Kwisatz Haderach to enable a second use of Kwisatz on the same round. Once you have started your Reveal Turn, you can’t change it to be an Agent Turn.
Source: BGG forum
                                                               * If you graft Chairdog to a card, but then trash Chairdog before your reveal turn, do you still return the other grafted card to your hand? Yes, the Chairdog puts an "installed effect" on the card it's grafted with, during the Agent turn when you play Chairdog. Even if you trash Chairdog before the Reveal turn, that effect remains installed on the other card.
Source: BGG Forum
Source: BGG Forum
                                                                  * If you graft Chairdog with Stillsuit Manufacturer, return Stillsuit Manufacturer to your hand, lose your Fremen alliance, and play it again on a future Agent turn, does Chairdog still recognize it as “the other grafted card” when it comes time for your Reveal turn (i.e. can you still use Stillsuit Manufacturer for the Reveal effect)? Unknown


Changing Allegiances
                                                                  * You may play this card for just one or for both of its effects.
Source: Message from designer.
                                                                  * You may not interrupt the two parts of this intrigue card with other player-initiated actions. 
Source: Message from designer.
                                                                  * As Lady Margot playing Changing Allegiances, can you shift influence to trigger your Loyalty bonus (2 spice from reaching 2 influence with BG), then use that spice toward the other part of the intrigue (paying 3 spice for a faction bump)? Yes.
Source: Message from designer.
                                                                  * As Y’rkoon playing Changing Allegiances, can you shift influence to trigger a Navigation card that gives you spice, and then use that spice to pay for the second part of Changing Allegiances? Yes. Intrigues must be executed “in one piece”, meaning there can’t be a player-initiated action in the middle of processing it. But the Navigation cards are “automatically triggered” not player-initiated.
Source: Message from designer.


Chani (leader)
                                                                     * During setup, place the Tactics token on the track printed on the track printed on the leader, in the space corresponding to the number of players. When the token reaches the rightmost space, reset it by returning it to the space you started on.
Source: Bloodlines rulebook
                                                                     * If you lose or retreat enough troops that you would pass the end of the Tactics track, you still reset at the starting space (and do not advance for those extra troops).
Source: Bloodlines rulebook
                                                                     * When resolving combat, troops that return to your supply are considered “lost.” Each different source of retreating or losing troops is handled separately.
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ
                                                                     * Can you push a troop in, retreat it with your Signet ring, and then immediately deploy it again the same turn? No, not unless you have a separate means of deploying it such as Detonation. “You can't deploy the same troop more than once from the same deployment source.”
Source: Message from designer 
                                                                     * Can you use the same physical cube twice toward fulfilling an intrigue card like Distraction or Coercive Negotiation (“when you deploy three or more units to the Conflict in a single turn”), if you deploy, retreat, and deploy that troop again (e.g. with Detonation)? 
No. “You need to have a moment in time when there are 3 units in the conflict that were deployed to the conflict this turn, then that requirement becomes true.”
Source: Message from designer 


Chaumurky
                                                                        * This Tech tile is an Endgame tiebreaker between players with the same number of Victory Points. (The amount of spice, Solari, water, and garrisoned troops those players have is irrelevant; the player with Chaumurky wins.)
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ


Choam Demands
                                                                           * Can you send this card to Accept Contract and immediately fulfill your new contract using this card’s agent box? Yes. 
Source: The card text itself. This card is an alternative means to complete a contract, substituting for the usual method. 
Discussion: BGG Forum


Choam Transports
                                                                           * The card draw from CHOAM Transports is triggered as soon as the contract is completed and can't be deferred.
Source: Message from designer
                                                                           * If you have a High Council contract, then visit High Council and purchase the Choam Transports tech, can you draw a card for completing your High Council contract?
Yes. Contract completion is just another effect that you process whenever you want before the end of the turn. If you wanted to avoid the card draw, you could choose to fulfill your contract before purchasing the tech.
Source: Hidden Assets Discord post


Clandestine Meeting
                                                                              * To play this card on an Agent turn, you must add at least one Agent icon to it somehow, (For example, by grafting it.)
Source: Immortality Rulebook


Combat Rewards Timing
                                                                                 * The rules do not indicate an order for players to take their combat rewards, and in most cases it does not matter. If it does matter (e.g. someone has knowledge of the top intrigue card, or multiple players have a choice or rewards), then resolve in the current round’s player turn order, starting with whoever is holding the First Player Token.
Source: Message from designer
See also: Combat Phase Timing sheet


Combat Symbol
(The crossed swords that appear on High Priority Travel and Occupation)
                                                                                    * You cannot stack this symbol (i.e. move 2+2 units from garrison to conflict if you send this card to a Combat board space)
Source: BGG Forum
                                                                                    * On High Priority Travel, you may select the Combat Symbol (instead of the Card Draw) even if you sent the card to a Combat board space. The symbol fizzles and provides no added effect.
Source: BGG Forum


Command
                                                                                       * Command effects (when you generate 6 or more Persuasion on Reveal) are mandatory. (The icon guide mistakenly uses the word “may”. The actual rulebook page is the correct one.)
Source: Message from designer
Source: BGG Forum


Conflict
                                                                                       * The rules for giving rewards in a Conflict are the same for one, two, or three players. They are different only for four players.
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ
                                                                                       * In a three player game, the 3rd place rewards are ignored, except in case of a tie for second.
Source: BGG Forum - includes examples of various tie scenarios


Contracts
                                                                                          * It is mandatory to complete a contract when you fulfill its conditions. When you have multiple contracts for the same board space, they are all completed in one turn, by sending one Agent to that space.
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ
                                                                                          * If a contract’s condition is sending an Agent to a board space, the contract is another effect of your Agent turn. You may carry out the contract effect in any order with the effects of the board space and the Agent box of the card you played.
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ
Source: Hidden Assets Discord post
                                                                                             * If you complete the Sardaukar contract that lets you Recall an Agent, during your first turn of the round, you are still required to complete the contract; the reward simply fizzles, since you have no other agent to recall.
Source: BGG Forum
                                                                                             * If you gain spice (e.g. from an Intrigue), then acquire a Harvest contract (e.g. from Leverage), then visit a Maker location, does that previous spice count toward the number needed to fulfill the Harvest contract? Yes.
Source: Message from designer 
                                                                                             * Does the “Earn Any Alliance” contract trigger if you already have an alliance when you get the contract? No. Whenever you take an alliance marker after taking the contract, the contract is completed.
Source: Bloodlines Rulebook
Source: BGG Forum
                                                                                                * If you are at 3 influence with the Emperor, and go to Dutiful Service acquiring the “Earn Any Alliance” contract, could you immediately fulfill it that turn? Yes, you choose the order of effects, so you can acquire the contract first, then take the Emperor bump.
Source: See “Timing of Effects”
Discussion:  BGG Forum


Corner the Market
                                                                                                   * Count the total number of The Spice Must Flow cards in your deck and your discard pile. Count each opponent’s The Spice Must Flow cards separately; if any one of them has more than you (or the same number as you), you don’t meet the second requirement of the Intrigue card. 
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ

Dreadnoughts
                                                                                                                                             * The base game rules require you to have a “troop” in the conflict for you to have strength, count your swords, and play Combat intrigue cards. A deployed dreadnought allows the same, even if you have it in a conflict without any troops. However, a dreadnought in your garrison does none of these things, and does not add strength to the conflict.
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ

ERRATA


Missionaria Protectiva — This card is part of the Bene Gesserit faction. Cards from the first English printing are missing this underneath its title. (If you have replaced the misprints, note that there should be only two copies of Missionaria Protectiva in your Imperium deck.)
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ


Rise of Ix Rulebook, page 4 — A tech tile with a “once per round” ability (indicated by the flip icon) can be used during one of your Agent or Reveal turns. The first English printing mistakenly said this could be done only during an Agent turn.
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ


Rise of Ix Rulebook, page 8 — This instruction for Setup of Solo & Two-Player Games is missing from the first English printing: Remove four of the original cards from the House Hagal deck: the two Hall of Oratory cards and the two Rally Troops cards.
Source: Official Dune Imperium FAQ


Uprising Rulebook, page 11 — The rulebook says "Whenever the Spy icon appears on a card or board space, you may place a Spy from your supply on an unoccupied observation post on the board." The word “may” is incorrectly used here. It is mandatory to place a Spy if you have at least one Spy in your supply.
